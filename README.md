# The Couples-Therapy Smoke Alarm

One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance: a smoke alarm that treats burnt toast as a relationship issue
instead of an emergency.

## The pitch

> The Couples-Therapy Smoke Alarm: instead of shrieking at burnt toast, it sighs 'we've talked about this' and quietly opens a window.

132 characters. The joke: the alarm is reframed as a weary couples
therapist, so burnt toast gets a sigh and an open window instead of a
shriek — the laugh comes from the invention itself, and the fix is silly
yet weirdly plausible. The pitch was picked from ideas brainstormed with
two AI helpers (Gemini and xKiro) and chosen for the freshest angle: a
smoke alarm doing relationship counselling.
